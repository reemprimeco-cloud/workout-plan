## Scope

The one public marketing page on the web (`/` for unauthenticated visitors).
Visitor mode: **Persuade**. Its only job is to send someone to the App Store;
there is no sign-up, no pricing and no checkout on the web.

Audience: someone in Kuwait or the Gulf with no relationship to the gym, who
found the listing cold. Action: download. Proof available: the product itself,
Arabic as a first language, and health-report-aware coaching. No testimonials,
metrics, press or before/after photography exist — none may be invented.

## Direction contract

THESIS: This page owns one idea — the coach answers *you*, and the answer is a
real plan, not a promise. It refuses the category arrangement: headline over
subcopy over a row of equal feature cards, with a phone mockup beside it.

OWN-WORLD: The Gulf Clinic as committed in DESIGN.md. Deep Harbour Navy carries
authority and the work blocks; Shallow Water Blue is the only actionable colour;
Sea Mist is the ground. Surface White cards at 16px with ambient navy-tinted
shadow. Inter for headings, Tajawal for body, both in every stack. RTL is the
ground state, English the override. Warmth appears only as signal.

STORY: The visitor reads a situation in someone's own Arabic words, sees the
coach return an actual plan card built around it, and understands that the
product reads their circumstances rather than filing them into a template. They
believe it was built for Arabic and for a real working day. They download.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A person's situation set at display scale in their own words,
right-aligned, in Deep Harbour Navy on Sea Mist. Immediately beneath it, offset
to the reading-left, the coach's reply rendered as a working plan card on
Surface White — exercises, restrictions and a desk-break note, legible at a
glance, not chat text. The App Store action sits inside this viewport beside the
card, in Shallow Water Blue, not deferred to the page's foot. The next
situation's first line is visible at the fold, so the rhythm is promised before
it is scrolled.

FORM: "The coach replying" — index 4 of seven ordered structures, dealt by the
roll (indices 7, 4, 2) and locked by the user over the dealt lead. Seed key
c5a1d180. The roll ran degraded: no catalog challengers and no quality-bar
boards were reachable.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the
finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its
provenance.
